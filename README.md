## 📖 Project Overview

This project was developed as part of a **Business Intelligence & Analytics Internship**.

The objective was to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights by combining **SQL**, **Excel**, and **Power BI**.

The dashboard enables decision-makers to monitor key business metrics, identify underperforming regions, analyze customer segments, and evaluate profitability across product categories.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

- Build an executive-level sales dashboard
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Compare regional sales performance
- Identify profitable product categories
- Analyze customer segments
- Support business decision-making through visual analytics

# 📊 Business Insights

### Regional Performance

- West region generated the highest sales.
- South region recorded the lowest sales and requires strategic improvement.

### Category Analysis

- Technology produced the highest profit.
- Furniture generated the lowest profitability.

### Customer Segments

- Consumer segment contributed over **50%** of total revenue.
- Corporate and Home Office segments followed respectively.

### Sales Trend

Monthly sales fluctuate throughout the year but demonstrate an overall upward business trend.

---

# 📈 Forecast Preview

Power BI Forecasting was used to generate future sales predictions with a **95% confidence interval**, providing an estimate of expected business performance based on historical sales trends.

---

# 🗂 Data Model

The project uses a single-table data model built on the **Sample Superstore** dataset.

The model serves as the foundation for all DAX calculations and dashboard visualizations.
